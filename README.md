Hi, I'm Jennifer. I teach and research special education transition in Kansas, and I build practical tools for students, educators, and small businesses.

- [Transition Assessment Toolkit](https://github.com/JJfww/TransitionAssessmentToolkit) — a searchable library of transition assessments and resources, organized by domain. [Visit the site →](https://jjfww.github.io/TransitionAssessmentToolkit/)
- [My Future Portfolio](https://github.com/JJfww/my-future-portfolio) — an accessible, student-led portfolio where students share their strengths and goals in their own words and present them at their IEP meeting. [Visit the site →](https://myfutureportfolio.com)
- [Accessible Learning](https://github.com/JJfww/accessible-learning) — a pilot AI plugin that reviews slides, documents, and courses for accessibility, Universal Design for Learning, and adult-learning quality.
- [Empowering Brew](https://github.com/jburnes-EBrew/empowering-brew) — an iPad sales and inventory tracker for a student-run coffee shop, with recipes, order-ahead, and daily backups.
- Etsy listing draft tools for [21 Glasses](https://jjfww.github.io/21Glasses-Digital-Asset-Draft-Tool/) and [GeekChic](https://jjfww.github.io/GeekChic-Digital-Listing-Draft-Tool/) — private helpers that draft digital listings for the shop owner to review and publish.
